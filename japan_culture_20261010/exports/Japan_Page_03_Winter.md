## English speaker notes

After the world of anime, I want to step into a real place that gives me a similar feeling.

The background here is Otaru Canal in Hokkaido. Snow lies along the water, and warm lights reflect on its surface. This is the kind of winter evening I would love to experience: a slow walk, a beautiful view, and no need to rush.

The video shows another winter town, Ginzan Onsen in Yamagata. It is a different place from Hokkaido. Its traditional wooden inns and snowy streets make it feel like a scene from a story. Onsen means hot spring, so the town also connects beautiful scenery with Japan’s bathing culture.

For me, these places show a quieter side of Japan. I would love to visit the lively cities, but I would also make time for snowy landscapes, small streets, and moments like these.

## 中文对照

看过动漫的世界之后，我想走进一个真实的地方，去寻找类似的感觉。

这里的背景是北海道的小樽运河。雪覆盖着水边，温暖的灯光倒映在水面上。这就是我很想体验的冬日傍晚：慢慢散步，看看风景，不必急着赶往下一个地方。

视频里是另一个冬日小镇——山形县的银山温泉。它和北海道是两个不同的地方。传统的木造旅馆和积雪的街道，让它像故事里的场景一样。“Onsen”就是温泉，所以这个地方也把美丽的风景和日本的温泉文化联系起来。

对我来说，这些地方展现了日本比较安静的一面。我想去热闹的城市，但也想为雪景、小巷和这样的时刻留出时间。
